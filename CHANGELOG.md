# Hearth changelog

Versioning: small updates and fixes go up by 0.01, very large feature batches by 0.1, until the 1.0 release.

## Beta 0.2.6
- New board on the chimney: a framed screen hung on the stone above the wreath, lit by the fire like the rest of the room
- Pin as many notes as you like from the phone; they rotate on the board, newest first, and stay until removed
- When nothing is pinned, the board shows your teams: live scores, results, and what's next (Raptors, Leafs, Blue Jays, TFC, Inter Miami, Barcelona, Argentina by default; change them on the phone). If a team is live while notes are up, the board alternates
- Admin page: pin/remove notes, see what's coming up, pick your teams

## Beta 0.2.5
- Stockings redone: each name is now stitched across a white fur cuff and baked into every frame of the fire video, so it moves exactly with its stocking (no more drift or sticker look). The garland ribbon still hangs in front of Gureet's stocking.
- The TV downloads the new 4K video from GitHub by itself and keeps it in its own storage; no USB step
- New loading screen: Hearth wordmark and a progress line, the room only appears once the video is really playing, then the corners settle in one by one
- Switching videos mid-session is a soft dip to black instead of a hard cut
- Colour buttons and number keys no longer do anything (no more accidental close-ups or demos); the yellow-button close-ups are gone
- New phone admin page (mann107.github.io/hearth/admin): live status updating every 2 seconds (what's playing, smoothness, response time, buffer, dropped frames, memory, uptime, elf, Santa, the TV's activity log) plus controls: volume, music, living light, Santa fly-by, Christmas Eve demo, elf, photos, notes to the TV, restart
- Pairing: the TV shows a QR code until a phone connects; hold OK on the remote to show it again

- Swinging stocking names now load from GitHub, so no USB step is needed

## Beta 0.12
- Removed the automatic camera drifts into the picture frames and mantel; the room now stays still
- Yellow button close-ups are now just the mantel and the tree

## Beta 0.11
- Stocking names are measured to each stocking's real centre line, letter-spaced to sit fully on the fabric, and slightly smaller
- Names now swing with their stockings: each stocking's sway was tracked across all 899 frames of the loop
- Elf: removed the three spots where he looked like he was floating; he now sits on the balcony's top rail and the coffee-table rim
- Crisper on-screen text (tight shadow instead of a soft glow, slightly heavier small type)

## Beta 0.1
- Install-once shell: the newest Hearth loads from GitHub each time the TV starts (saved copy when offline, built-in copy as a last resort)
- Video and music read from the USB stick, then stored on the TV for instant starts
- Fireplace scene in 4K with its own 3-hour soundtrack
- Stocking names, family photos in the two picture frames, memories close-ups
- Living light (fire glow, twinkling fairy lights), Santa past the windows, Santa over Brampton
- Elf hide-and-seek, globe Santa tracker, look-closer close-ups
- Status screen (Info button)
