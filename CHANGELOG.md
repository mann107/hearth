# Hearth changelog

Versioning: small updates and fixes go up by 0.01, very large feature batches by 0.1, until the 1.0 release.

## Beta 0.13
- Swinging stocking names now load from GitHub, so no USB step is needed

## Beta 0.12
- Removed the automatic camera drifts into the picture frames and mantel; the room now stays still
- Yellow button close-ups are now just the mantel and the tree

## Beta 0.11
- Stocking names are measured to each stocking's real centre line, letter-spaced to sit fully on the fabric, and slightly smaller
- Names now swing with their stockings: each stocking's sway was tracked across all 899 frames of the loop
- Elf: removed the three spots where he looked like he was floating; he now sits on the balcony's top rail and the coffee-table rim
- Crisper on-screen text (tight shadow instead of a soft glow, slightly heavier small type)

## Beta 0.1
- Install-once shell: the newest Hearth loads from GitHub each time the TV starts (saved copy when offline, built-in copy as a last resort)
- Video and music read from the USB stick, then stored on the TV for instant starts
- Fireplace scene in 4K with its own 3-hour soundtrack
- Stocking names, family photos in the two picture frames, memories close-ups
- Living light (fire glow, twinkling fairy lights), Santa past the windows, Santa over Brampton
- Elf hide-and-seek, globe Santa tracker, look-closer close-ups
- Status screen (Info button)
